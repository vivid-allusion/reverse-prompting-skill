# The Last Ferry

A short film, 1958, the Portuguese coast. Three scenes.

1. **EXT. HARBOUR - DAWN.** The last ferry of the season loads at the quay. Harbour workers swing crates aboard.
2. **INT. FERRY CABIN - DAY.** MARTA (60s), in her late husband's grey wool coat, sits on the bench beside TOMÁS (10), her grandson. She opens a small tin box: his letters. Tomás watches her, then looks away to the sea.
3. **EXT. ISLAND JETTY - DUSK.** Marta stands alone at the end of the jetty, the tin box closed in her hands.
